Maps of The Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands and
City of Dalaran (this folder) are by Santiago Reyes, from his project
Atlas de Azeroth: Forever. Used in WalkMap with his permission, with credit
and links to his support pages.

They are his own reinterpretations, not official World of Warcraft artwork,
and he is not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. His watermark stays on
every map; the pictures are only converted to the game's texture format,
never cropped, altered or drawn into.
